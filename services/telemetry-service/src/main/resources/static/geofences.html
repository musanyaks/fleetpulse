Geofences

Module on the roadmap — geofence-service will store zones, evaluate every GPS point on telemetry.raw, and emit enter/exit/dwell events.

Planned zone types

• Depot / warehouse (arrival & departure notifications)

• Customer sites (auto trip milestones)

• Restricted zones (instant CRITICAL alert)

• Dwell limits (vehicle idle in zone > N minutes)

Evaluation design

• Point-in-polygon per reading, zones cached in-memory per vehicle partition

• Hysteresis (2 consecutive points inside/outside) to prevent flapping at boundaries

• Events to vehicle.events; map overlay rendered on this page + Overview

• Nairobi demo zones: Westlands depot, Industrial Area restricted, JKIA no-stop
